Pulsar Support

AI drafts. Rules decide. You approve every refund.

Pulsar reads your support email, checks each claim against your own order system, and drafts a reply where every fact links to its source. Anything involving money goes to a person.

  • Every fact in a draft is a chip. Click one to see where it came from.
  • Refunds always need a human. Over an approver's limit, they route to the Owner.
  • Suspicious mail is quarantined before any AI reads it.
